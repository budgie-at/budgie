Budgie is an expense tracker that works the way your money actually moves: offline, instantly, and without asking anyone's permission. Open it on the platform, in the queue, on a plane — every expense lands in under five seconds, and nothing waits for a signal. No account to create, no bank credentials to hand over, no subscription clock ticking in the background.

WORKS WITHOUT A CONNECTION
Every transaction lives on your phone. Add expenses, browse your history, and review your analytics with no internet connection at all — nothing has to reach a server first.

EVERY EXPENSE IN SECONDS
Say an expense out loud or type it, and Budgie fills in the category on its own, right there on your device. Split a purchase across categories or people in the same flow.

SEE WHERE THE MONEY GOES
Spending analytics break down every category and tag, so a pattern shows up before it becomes a problem. Track your net worth across every account, card, and currency in one place.

BUDGETS AND RUNWAY
Set a budget that matches your payday, not the calendar month, and watch your runway update as you spend, so you always know how far your money will stretch.

ACCOUNTS, CARDS AND CRYPTO
Track cash, cards, and crypto holdings side by side in whatever currency they're actually in — Budgie converts on the fly. Where your bank supports it, import statements directly instead of typing every line by hand.

DEBTS AND SHARED MONEY
Keep a running ledger of who owes who, log repayments, and see the real remaining balance at a glance.

LOCKED DOWN
Unlock with a PIN, your face, or your fingerprint, and hide your balances from the app switcher and screenshots. Back up your data on your own terms, protected the whole time.

EVERYTHING ELSE YOU'D WANT
• Multi-currency accounts and automatic conversion
• Recurring payments calendar so nothing sneaks up on you
• Import bank statements as CSV or PDF, where supported
• Tags for spending you track your own way
• Voice entry when typing isn't convenient
• PIN, biometric lock, and screenshot protection
• No account, no bank login, no subscription
