Your money is personal. Keep it that way.

Budgie helps you track expenses, stay within your budget and see your whole financial picture, without creating an account. Your spending history lives on your device, ready when you need it, even without a connection.

SPEND WITH A CLEARER PICTURE
• Log expenses, income and transfers across cash, cards and accounts.
• Make categories and tags your own, and split purchases across categories.
• See where your money goes with spending charts, category breakdowns and date filters.

PLAN WHAT COMES NEXT
• Set a monthly budget with category limits and see what is left.
• Enable budget alerts as you approach or reach your limits.
• Estimate how long your money could last with runway, based on your transaction history.
• See detected recurring charges and subscriptions in a calendar.

LESS TYPING, MORE CONTROL
• Use suggestions from past transactions to log familiar expenses.
• Apply rules to organize transactions and review uncategorized spending together.
• Enable on-device AI for category and tag suggestions, plus voice entry. Speech recognition and AI processing run locally after the models download.

ONE VIEW, MANY CURRENCIES
• Track cash, bank accounts, crypto, deposits and debts.
• View balances and net worth in your base currency using exchange rates.
• Keep budget and net worth summaries close with Home Screen widgets.

BRING YOUR HISTORY WITH YOU
• Import transactions from CSV files and supported bank statements, including Erste PDFs and PrivatBank exports.
• Connect Monobank or Binance with your own API credentials if you choose. Manual tracking needs no bank connection.
• Export transactions to CSV and create database backups you control.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your ledger stays on your device. Enable a PIN to encrypt the database with SQLCipher, add biometric unlock and turn on screenshot protection. Backups retain the database encryption when a PIN is set.

Everyday tracking, budgets and analytics work offline. Bank sync, exchange-rate updates and AI model downloads need internet access. AI is optional and requires device storage for its models.

Free to use. No Budgie account. No subscription.
