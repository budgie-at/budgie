Your money is personal. Now you choose who shares it.

Budgie is an offline-first expense tracker and budget planner. No Budgie account, no subscription and no Budgie server holding your transactions. Your ledger lives on your iPhone, and through your own iCloud it follows you to your other devices and to the people you share it with.

NEW: BUDGET TOGETHER AS A FAMILY
• Choose which accounts and budgets to share with your partner or family through iCloud. They can see and edit what you share. Everything else stays private.
• Your own iPhone and iPad stay in sync automatically, even after days offline.
• Automatic iCloud backups, with every record encrypted on your device before it leaves.

NEW: SCAN A RECEIPT, DONE
• Point the camera at a receipt, or pick one from Photos or Files. Text recognition runs on your iPhone, fills in a ready-to-save expense and applies your rules.
• Capture Apple Pay taps automatically with a Shortcuts automation.

NEW: EU BANK SYNC, WITH NO MIDDLEMAN
• Connect EU banks such as N26 and Trade Republic through your own Enable Banking app. Your phone talks to the bank directly. There is no Budgie server in between.
• Monobank and Binance sync too, including Binance card purchases, Earn rewards and staking.
• Import CSV files with columns matched automatically, Erste PDF statements, PrivatBank exports and Inzhur fund statements.

NEW: ASK SIRI
"How much did I spend on groceries?" Siri answers with your spending, what is left in your budget, account balances, net worth and today's upcoming charges, without opening the app. Amounts stay hidden while your PIN lock is on.

BUDGETS THAT LOOK AHEAD
• Weekly, biweekly, monthly or yearly budgets with category limits, and rollover for what you did not spend.
• Safe to spend: one daily figure that adjusts as you go, plus a forecast that warns you before a category runs over.
• Set money aside for yearly bills with sinking funds, suggested from your recurring payments.
• Separate budgets for trips and events, and budgets based on your income.
• Auto-tune limits from how you actually spend.

NEVER MISS A RECURRING CHARGE
• Subscriptions, bills and recurring income are detected automatically and shown as a list or a calendar.
• Add a recurring payment yourself. Budgie matches the charges that follow.

LESS TYPING, MORE CONTROL
• Clear uncategorized spending in a grouped inbox, a few taps per group.
• Suggestions from your history, rules, splits, refunds and pay-in-parts purchases.
• Optional on-device AI for category and tag suggestions and voice entry. Speech recognition and AI run locally after the models download.

ONE VIEW, EVERY CURRENCY
• Cash, cards, bank accounts, crypto, deposits, investment funds and debts in one net worth, in your base currency.
• Spending charts, category trends and a runway that shows how long your money could last.
• Home Screen widgets for your budget and net worth.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Set a PIN and your database is encrypted with SQLCipher. Add Face ID, screenshot protection and a privacy mode that masks amounts. Export to CSV or keep a full backup file you control.

Everyday tracking, budgets and analytics work offline. iCloud sync, bank sync, exchange rates and AI model downloads need internet access. AI is optional and needs device storage for its models.

Free to use. No Budgie account. No subscription.
